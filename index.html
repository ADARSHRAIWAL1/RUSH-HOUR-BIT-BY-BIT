How you are judged

100 points in total, with the weight of each criterion shown up front.

The 3-report system

You submit three short reports during the event. Each one captures where your team stands and why you made your choices.

Report 1

Your idea, your first assumptions and your plan.

Report 2

What you validated, what changed after the Rush shocks, and how you adapted.

Report 3

What you built, what you learned and where you would take it next.

RushHour is not only about the final product. The decisions you made, how you adapted and how you improved matter just as much.

Rules & guidelines

Participants

  • Respectful conduct at all times.
  • No pre-built templates or projects.
  • Overnight zone restrictions apply.
  • Only allowed resources may be used.
  • Stick to your registered team.

Judges

  • Score only against the published rubric.
  • Declare any conflict of interest.
  • Keep Rush details confidential.
  • Give every team equal time and respect.

Late submissions

If a report misses its deadline, a penalty is applied as explained in the rulebook. Exact penalty to be confirmed.

Questions

What if my team can't code well?

Coding is not compulsory. Non-technical participants are welcome because the event is about more than code.

What happens if we miss a report deadline?

Late reports lose marks as set out in the rulebook. Details will be confirmed before the event.

Can our team have fewer than 3 members?

No. Teams must have 3 or 4 members.

Is food or stay provided overnight?

Overnight food and stay arrangements will be confirmed with the registered teams before the event.
